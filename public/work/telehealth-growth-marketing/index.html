01 / The assignment

Make different acquisition channels behave like one growth system.

The contract covered LinkedIn, paid AdWords and Google Grant campaigns for five Telehealth.org landing pages. Performance depended on both the traffic source and what happened after the click.

02 / The acquisition system

Four connected components. One commercial return.

Each channel had a distinct job, but none was managed in isolation.

  1. 01Paid search

    Capture active demand from people already looking for telehealth education.

  2. 02Google Grant

    Extend qualified reach while keeping campaigns and landing pages relevant.

  3. 03LinkedIn

    Reach professional audiences in a focused B2B environment.

  4. 04Landing pages

    Turn traffic into a clear and measurable action across five destinations.

03 / Operating principle

Media buying and conversion design worked as one discipline.

Search and display activity was managed alongside grant and LinkedIn acquisition. Campaign structure, audience targeting and landing-page experience were refined together to improve control and commercial relevance.
