01 / Challenge

Profitable growth needed better signals, not simply more spend.

The campaign focused on Pakistan's healthcare-apparel market. The objective was to grow e-commerce revenue while improving acquisition efficiency, supported by stronger tracking and a disciplined way to identify scalable audience-and-creative combinations.

Market
Pakistan
Audience
Healthcare professionals and medical students
Objective
Profitable e-commerce purchases

02 / Strategy and execution

Every decision followed the purchase signal.

  1. 01 Test

    Audience and creative combinations

    Healthcare audiences and product-led messaging around value, comfort, quality and utility were tested rather than assuming one winning formula.

  2. 02 Read

    Purchase efficiency and signal quality

    Conversion behaviour was reviewed alongside website and event data to strengthen tracking confidence and judge commercial quality.

  3. 03 Reduce

    Weak combinations lost budget

    Campaigns, ad sets and creative directions that failed to justify spend were reduced or paused.

  4. 04 Scale

    Stronger performers earned more

    Budget moved progressively toward combinations delivering better purchase efficiency.

03 / Customer learning

Useful product truth converted attention into intent.

Practical, professional and product-specific messaging mattered. Quality, comfort, usability and professional appearance gave buyers clearer reasons to consider the product.
