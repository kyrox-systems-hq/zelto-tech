01 / Commercial brief

Drive profitable purchases while making the account easier to scale with confidence.

The campaign focused on Pakistan's healthcare-apparel market, promoting MedsAttire's medical workwear across scrubs and related categories. The commercial objective was straightforward: grow e-commerce revenue while improving acquisition efficiency rather than buying growth at any cost.

The account also needed stronger tracking confidence and a more disciplined way to identify scalable audience-and-creative combinations. That made measurement, testing and budget decisions part of the growth problem, not back-office details.

03 / Optimisation loop

Test broadly. Cut weak signals. Reallocate to what converts.

TestAudience × creative combinations

Different audiences and product-focused messaging were put into market rather than assuming one winning formula.

ReadPurchase efficiency and signal quality

Performance analysis centred on conversion behaviour and the commercial quality of the result.

ReducePause weaker performers

Campaigns, ad sets or creative directions that failed to justify spend were reduced instead of being protected by sunk-cost thinking.

ScaleMove budget toward winners

Budget followed stronger purchase efficiency, creating a repeatable process for controlled scaling.

04 / Customer learning

Healthcare buyers respond to useful product truth.

The campaign reinforced a simple pattern: practical, professional and product-specific messaging matters. Quality, comfort, usability and professional appearance all contribute to purchase consideration, especially when product presentation makes those benefits easy to understand.
