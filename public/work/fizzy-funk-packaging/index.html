Fizzy Funk

A vibrant fruit-drink packaging system built across four flavours and a complete launch world.

Packaging designFlavour systemProduct mock-upsCampaign visuals

01 / The direction

A flavour-led packaging family designed to feel energetic, playful and unmistakable.

Each flavour uses its own colour, fruit character and illustration while retaining a common logo, layout and graphic rhythm. The result is a range that feels varied on shelf without losing the recognition of one master brand.

The work extends beyond the cans into flat label artwork, product mock-ups and launch-ready imagery, allowing the packaging system to support both retail presentation and campaign creative.
