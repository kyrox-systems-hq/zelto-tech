01 / The direction

Turning a complex AI proposition into one recognisable visual system.

The work uses deep navy and vibrant lime green to create immediate recognition in a crowded B2B technology market. Angular forms, digital patterns and high-contrast compositions give the brand a sense of movement without relying on generic futuristic imagery.

The system was developed to travel across a wide range of formats, from event displays and platform campaigns to social media content, sales storytelling and mobile-first creative.
