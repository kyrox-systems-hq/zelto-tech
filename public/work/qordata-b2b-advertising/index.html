01 / Commercial context

A click only mattered if the person behind it could become a customer.

The assignment was less about buying reach and more about connecting audience quality, active intent and conversion into one demand-generation system for specialist compliance software.

02 / The demand system

Four moving parts. One commercial objective.

Each part of the journey earned its place by moving relevant prospects closer to qualified demand.

  1. 01LinkedIn

    Reach decision-makers in the roles most relevant to the buying context.

  2. 02Google Search

    Capture active demand from prospects already looking for a solution.

  3. 03Lead magnets

    Give paid attention a useful reason to become identifiable demand.

  4. 04Retargeting

    Bring relevant visitors back into the journey after the first click.

03 / Operating principle

Commercial relevance came before scale.

Campaign decisions were based on whether audiences matched the buying context, not whether impressions were easy to generate. Lead quality became the lens for audience, channel and conversion decisions.

04 / What changed

The campaign became a connected B2B acquisition journey.

The result reflects stronger commercial lead quality, supported by tighter audience selection, clearer conversion assets and continuity after the first visit.
