01 / The problem

Traffic was only useful if the right business buyers moved further into the funnel.

The work sat inside a B2B compliance-software context, where a large click count means little if the people arriving have no buying relevance. The campaign therefore needed to connect channel targeting with conversion quality.

LinkedIn created access to defined decision-maker audiences while Google Search captured active demand. Lead magnets and retargeting then gave interested prospects a clearer path to re-engage.

03 / Result

The system increased qualified leads by 17%.

The important point was not that more people entered the top of the funnel. More of the people moving through it matched the commercial definition of a useful lead.

That is the operating principle the case study demonstrates: acquisition channels, conversion assets and retargeting work better when they are judged as one demand-generation system.
