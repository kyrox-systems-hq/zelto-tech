01 / Commercial context

In B2B compliance software, a click is only useful if the person behind it can become a customer.

The assignment was therefore less about buying reach and more about connecting audience quality, intent and conversion into one demand-generation system.

Product contextCommercial compliance software

A specialist B2B proposition where relevance matters more than reach.

Buyer challengeReach decision-makers with genuine intent

Paid media had to attract the right commercial roles, not simply produce cheap traffic.

Measurement ruleOptimise for qualified leads

Lead quality became the primary lens for judging campaign performance.

02 / The demand system

Four moving parts, one commercial objective.

LinkedIn created controlled access to relevant roles. Search captured active demand. Lead magnets gave that demand a useful next step. Retargeting brought qualified interest back into the funnel.

03 / What changed

The work moved the campaign from channel activity to a connected B2B acquisition journey.

Audience strategy

Commercial relevance before scale.

Campaign decisions were based on whether the audience matched the buying context, not whether impressions were easy to generate.

Conversion

Stronger lead magnets

Paid traffic had a clearer next step and more reason to identify itself.

Re-engagement

Retargeting added continuity

Relevant visitors could return to the funnel rather than being treated as one-click traffic.

increase in qualified leads

04 / Result

17% more qualified leads.

The case demonstrates the value of treating paid media, conversion assets and retargeting as one demand system instead of three disconnected tactics.
