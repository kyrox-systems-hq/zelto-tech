Refined Vision

Product design that turns eyewear into a complete campaign world.

Product visualisationArt directionEditorial designSocial campaign

01 / The direction

A product concept designed together with the world around it.

Rather than treating the glasses as an isolated object, the project develops the product and its campaign language together. Cobalt blue, fluorescent lime and sharp editorial layouts create a confident identity that feels premium without becoming predictable.

The visual system moves between product-led imagery, lifestyle photography and campaign messaging, allowing the same design direction to work across launch material, social content and retail storytelling.
