Site-wide image rules

Sharp at the size it needs to be. Never large simply to fill the page.

01No forced crop

Artwork and composite boards use their natural aspect ratio and object-fit: contain.

02No upscaling

The rendered width stays within the useful resolution of the source asset.

03Measured maximums

Project heroes cap around 760px. Gallery images cap according to their native dimensions.

04Correct loading

The hero loads eagerly. Below-the-fold images retain dimensions and load lazily.

05Honest fallback

Use an initials mark when a profile image is too small to remain sharp.
