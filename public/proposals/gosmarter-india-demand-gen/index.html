Why now

The commercial signal is unusually clear.

Nightingale HQ announced on 9 July 2026 that its angel round will support growth across the UK, Ireland and India. Campaign architecture

Lead with the operational problem, then let the product proof do the selling.

The campaign should not ask a production or quality leader to understand a broad AI platform first. It should begin with the paper trail, traceability or scrap problem they already recognise, then route them into the GoSmarter capability and proof that matches it.

Message tests

Three campaign angles from the same product truth.

The aim is not to invent new promises. It is to make the existing value proposition easier to recognise for different buyer problems, then test which message earns the strongest qualified response in the new market.

Compliance angle

Stop rebuilding the audit trail at the end of the job.

Lead with automatic cert linking, heat-number traceability and searchable despatch records for teams currently carrying the documentation burden.

Best fit: quality and compliance leaders
Operations angle

Less retyping. More metal moving.

Position GoSmarter around the time lost to manual certificate handling, stock records and disconnected production admin.

Best fit: production and operations
Yield angle

Cut metal, not margin.

Use the existing cutting optimisation capability and published scrap-reduction proof to open conversations where material yield is commercially significant.

Best fit: fabricators and service centres
Offer and capture

Give colder traffic a useful middle step.

Not every manufacturer arriving from search, LinkedIn or an industry partner will be ready to book a demo. A small diagnostic resource can convert interest earlier while also revealing which operational problem matters most.

Proposed lead magnet

Metal Traceability Readiness Check

A short practical assessment that helps a production or quality team identify where certificate handling, stock records and despatch documentation are still disconnected.
